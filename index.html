QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know regarding materials, laptop sizing, shipping & returns.

What material is used in AddoIndia bags? ▼
All AddoIndia bags are crafted using ultra-fine microfiber vegan saffiano leather. It is 100% cruelty-free, water-resistant, scratch-resistant, and significantly lighter than traditional animal hides while offering identical luxury touch and durability.
Which AddoIndia bags can fit a 14" or 16" laptop? ▼
The Ava Tote and The Elara Tote are specifically designed with a padded sleeve that comfortably accommodates laptops up to 14 inches. For larger 15.6" or 16" MacBook Pro models, The Harper Work Tote provides dedicated padding and reinforced strap reinforcement.
What is your delivery timeframe & return policy? ▼
We offer complimentary express delivery across India (2-4 business days for metro cities, 4-6 days for rest of India). We provide a hassle-free 7-day exchange and return policy on all unworn items in original packaging.
Do you offer extra discounts on prepaid orders? ▼
Yes! Prepaid orders (UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, NetBanking) automatically qualify for extra savings with code PREPAID500, plus instant priority dispatch from our fulfillment hub.
